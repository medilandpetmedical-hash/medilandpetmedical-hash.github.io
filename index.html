What to expect

Your first visit, in three steps.

  1. Walk in or message us

    Come by during opening hours, or send a WhatsApp message first to check stock or book grooming.

  2. Tell us what your pet needs

    Bring the prescription or describe the problem. We help you find the right medicine, food or accessory.

  3. Take it home, or book a groom
